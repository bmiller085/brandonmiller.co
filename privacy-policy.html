Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Brandon Miller (“I,” “me,” or “my”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information collected through brandonmiller.co (the “Site”).

This Privacy Policy describes how I collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information — including information collected through contact forms, consulting inquiry intake, and payment processing on this Site. It also explains the privacy rights available to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (collectively, the “CCPA”).

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the Site, including when you:

  • visit or browse the Site;
  • submit an inquiry through a contact or consulting intake form;
  • purchase the book or other products through the Site;
  • submit payment information in connection with consulting services;
  • communicate with me by email or through the Site; or
  • interact with Site features that use cookies or analytics tools.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected offline, through third-party websites or services not operated by me, or through third-party platforms linked from the Site.

Payment processing is handled by third-party processors (such as Stripe or PayPal). I do not store your full payment card information on my systems. Please review the applicable processor’s privacy policy for information about how payment data is handled.

2. Personal Information We Collect

I may collect the following categories of personal information through the Site:

A. Identifiers

Examples include:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • billing address;
  • internet protocol (IP) address; and
  • other online identifiers associated with your browser or device.

B. Financial and Payment Information

When you submit payment for consulting services or book purchases, payment processing is handled by a third-party processor. I may receive limited transaction-related information such as:

  • transaction confirmation and reference numbers;
  • partial payment card information (e.g., last four digits and card type) provided by the payment processor; and
  • billing name and address associated with the transaction.

I do not receive or store full payment card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account details. Full payment information is collected and processed directly by the applicable third-party payment processor.

C. Commercial and Consulting Inquiry Information

Examples include:

  • the contents of messages and inquiries submitted through contact or intake forms;
  • organization name, job title, and professional details you voluntarily provide;
  • details about the nature of your consulting need or project; and
  • records of communications related to consulting engagements.

D. Internet or Other Electronic Network Activity Information

Examples include:

  • browsing activity on the Site;
  • pages viewed and referral sources;
  • approximate location based on IP address;
  • device and browser information; and
  • technical information about your visit recorded in server logs maintained by my website hosting provider.

E. Professional or Business Information

If you contact me regarding consulting services, I may collect professional or business-related details you voluntarily provide, such as your organization, industry, or compliance-related context.

I do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the Site, such as government identification numbers, precise geolocation, health information, or similar sensitive data.

F. Email Subscription Information

If you subscribe to my email list through the Site, I collect your email address, date and page where you signed up and, if you provide it, your name.

3. Sources of Personal Information

I collect personal information from the following categories of sources:

  • Directly from you, when you fill out a contact or intake form, submit a payment, email me, or otherwise communicate with me;
  • Automatically from your device or browser, through server log files maintained by my website hosting provider (GitHub Pages); and
  • From third-party payment processors (such as Stripe or PayPal), who provide transaction confirmation and related information when you submit payment through the Site.

4. How We Use Personal Information

I may use personal information for the following business and commercial purposes:

  • to send you the free checklist, book announcements, and related professional content if you subscribe to my email list;
  • to respond to your inquiries and communicate with you;
  • to process and fulfill payments for consulting services or book purchases;
  • to manage consulting engagements and related client records;
  • to provide information about the book, consulting services, and related professional content;
  • to operate, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • to monitor Site performance, traffic, and visitor engagement;
  • to detect, prevent, and address security issues, fraud, or misuse;
  • to maintain business records and internal administration;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

I use personal information only as reasonably necessary and proportionate for these purposes.

5. Payment Processing

Payment processing for consulting services and book purchases is handled by third-party payment processors. Depending on the payment method selected, your payment may be processed through:

  • Stripe; or
  • PayPal.

When you submit payment information, you are transmitting that information directly to the applicable payment processor through a secure connection. I do not receive, store, or have access to your full payment card number, CVV, or bank account details.

I may receive transaction confirmation data, including partial card information, billing name, billing address, and transaction amount, as provided by the processor to confirm successful payment.

Each payment processor has its own privacy policy governing the collection and use of payment data. I encourage you to review the applicable processor’s privacy policy before submitting payment information.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a static website hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. The Site itself does not set cookies. GitHub may collect technical information, such as visitors’ IP addresses, in its server logs for security and operational purposes.

Fonts and other Site files are served directly from the Site, not from third-party content networks.

I do not currently use:

  • Google Analytics;
  • Meta Pixel;
  • advertising retargeting tools;
  • session replay tools; or
  • similar third-party advertising technologies.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Third-party services you choose to interact with, such as the email signup and contact forms described below, may use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies.

6A. Email Subscribers

If you subscribe to my email list through the Site, I collect your email address. I use it to send you the free checklist, news about the book, and related professional content. Emails are sent through Kit (kit.com), which acts as a service provider on my behalf. Kit’s tools can show whether an email was opened and whether its links were clicked. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, and I will honor your request promptly. I do not sell or rent my email list.

7. How We Disclose Personal Information

I do not sell your personal information.

I do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

I may disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients for business purposes:

  • Website hosting and platform providers, including GitHub (GitHub Pages);
  • Kit, which delivers my emails;
  • Third-party payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), to process and confirm payments;
  • Email and productivity service providers, including Formspree, which receives contact form submissions and forwards them to me, and systems used to maintain related records;
  • Cloud-based document or productivity providers, including Google Workspace, where inquiry or engagement information may be stored for internal business use; and
  • Legal, compliance, or governmental entities, when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect my rights, property, or safety.

I do not knowingly disclose personal information to data brokers.

8. Data Retention

I retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

In general, I use the following retention guidelines:

  • Email list information is kept until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it;
  • Contact form submissions and general inquiries: up to 3 years after our last substantive communication;
  • Consulting engagement records and related correspondence: up to 5 years after the conclusion of the engagement, or longer if required for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate business recordkeeping;
  • Payment transaction records: as required by applicable tax, accounting, or financial recordkeeping obligations, typically up to 7 years;
  • Analytics and cookie-related information: for the period made available through the website platform or analytics settings, or as otherwise reasonably necessary for Site administration; and
  • Legal or compliance records: as long as required by applicable law, regulation, or legitimate business need.

I may retain information for a longer period when necessary to investigate issues, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, or protect my rights.

9. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the following rights under the CCPA, subject to certain exceptions and limitations:

A. Right to Know

You may request that I disclose:

  • the categories of personal information collected about you;
  • the categories of sources from which the personal information was collected;
  • the business or commercial purposes for collecting personal information;
  • the categories of third parties to whom personal information is disclosed; and
  • the specific pieces of personal information collected about you.

B. Right to Delete

You may request that I delete personal information collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions.

C. Right to Correct

You may request that I correct inaccurate personal information I maintain about you.

D. Right to Non-Discrimination

I will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights under California law.

E. Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing

I do not sell personal information and do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

F. Right to Limit Use and Disclosure of Sensitive Personal Information

I do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that would trigger a right to limit under California law.

10. How to Exercise Your Privacy Rights

To submit a privacy rights request, please contact me using the information in Section 15 below.

Please describe your request with enough detail for me to understand, evaluate, and respond.

I may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests. Verification may include matching the information you provide with information in my records. If I cannot verify your identity, I may deny the request as permitted by law.

You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf, subject to applicable verification requirements.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, including booksellers and professional organizations. I am not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party sites or services.

If you access a third-party site through a link on this Site, please review that site’s privacy policy before submitting personal information.

12. Children’s Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 16, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through the Site. If I learn that I have collected personal information from a child under 16 without appropriate authorization, I will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

13. Security

I use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information collected through the Site. Payment information submitted through the Site is transmitted via secure, encrypted connections managed by the applicable third-party payment processor.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to my practices, technologies, legal requirements, or other operational needs. When I do, I will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this policy.
