About the book

A growing company brings on five “independent contractors” to handle customer service. They work set hours, use company equipment, and report to a manager. No one questions it for two years. Then one of those workers is let go, files for unemployment, and the state rules they were an employee all along. Now the company owes back payroll taxes, penalties, and overtime it never planned for.

This happens every week, in businesses of every size, often without anyone recognizing the risk in the moment. Hiring, firing, pay, leave, accommodations, investigations: these aren’t administrative tasks. They’re legal decisions, and many of the people making them were never trained to see it that way.

HR Compliance: A Practical Guide to Managing the Challenges in a Complex Legal Environment closes that gap. It’s written for HR professionals, business owners, and executives and operations leaders, and it turns nearly two decades of real compliance program-building into a framework you can actually run, not legal theory.

Inside, you’ll find

A framework you can actually run

  • A practical framework for building a compliance program from the ground up
  • Sample templates and checklists you can adapt immediately
  • Plain-language guidance on writing policy, training your people, auditing your practices, and responding when something goes wrong
  • A clear look at where HR compliance is headed next

Compliance is a marathon, not a sprint. This is your training plan.

Who is this book for?

Written for the people doing the real work

HR Professionals

Whether you’re a generalist wearing ten hats or an experienced compliance specialist, this book gives you a practical, domain-by-domain framework for managing the legal complexities that land on your desk every day, without leaning on outside counsel for everything.

Small & Mid-Size Business Owners

If you’re running a growing company and handling HR yourself, or with a small team, this book gives you the compliance foundation you need. Written by a practitioner, not an academic, in language that’s actually usable.

Executives & Operations Leaders

You don’t need a law degree to protect your organization from compliance risk. You need clear, actionable guidance written for the people doing the real work. That’s exactly what this book delivers.

A note from the author

I didn’t write this book from a distance. Every chapter reflects real situations I’ve encountered or know others who have—real decisions under real pressure, with real stakes. I wrote the book I wish I’d had early in my career: one that treats compliance not as a checkbox exercise, but as a core competency that protects people and organizations alike.

My hope is that this becomes a resource you return to. Dog-eared, highlighted, and genuinely useful.
